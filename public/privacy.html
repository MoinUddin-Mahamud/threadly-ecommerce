Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") collects when you use this marketplace, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Order details: delivery name, phone number, delivery address, the items you bought and the order total.
  • Payment status: whether and how an order was paid. Card and wallet details are handled by the payment provider (such as SSLCommerz, Stripe, PayPal, Flutterwave or Mollie) and are not stored by us. For bank or mobile-banking transfers we keep the payment reference you send.
  • Technical data needed to run the site, such as a session cookie that keeps you logged in and your shopping cart saved in your browser.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, send order and delivery emails, give sellers the details they need to ship the items you bought from them, prevent fraud and abuse, answer your questions and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Sellers receive your name, phone number and delivery address for the orders that contain their items, once the order is confirmed. Delivery couriers and payment providers receive what they need to do their job. Order, delivery and account emails are sent through the email service we use ([EMAIL PROVIDER]), which therefore handles your email address and the content of those emails. We do not sell your personal information.

AI assistant

If the chat assistant is switched on, the messages you type are sent to the AI provider we have chosen ([AI PROVIDER]) so that it can write a reply. Please do not type passwords, card details or other sensitive information into the chat. So that the assistant can follow the conversation, the last few messages are kept in the server's memory under a random tl_chat cookie for up to 30 minutes. They are not saved in our database.

Cookies and local storage

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in. It is set when you log in and lasts up to 24 hours.
  • tl_chat cookie: set only if you use the chat assistant. A random code that lasts 30 minutes and is sent only to the chat.
  • lang cookie: remembers the language you chose.
  • Browser storage: your shopping cart, your saved-items list (when you are not signed in), your language and theme choice, the shop name for faster display, and a temporary code that stops an order from being placed twice. These stay in your browser and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, refunds and legal requirements, and keep account details until you ask us to delete the account.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to us at the address on the contact page.
