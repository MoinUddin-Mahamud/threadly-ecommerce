Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Choose your items and size, add them to the cart, and check out. You can order as a guest or with an account. Orders placed as a guest appear under My orders once you have verified the email address of your account.

Which payment methods can I use?

The methods available are shown at checkout. Depending on how the store is set up, these can include local online payment (SSLCommerz), international cards and wallets (Stripe, PayPal, Flutterwave, Mollie), Cash on Delivery, and bank or mobile-banking transfer.

Why is my order still "awaiting confirmation"?

Cash on Delivery orders are confirmed by our team, usually with a phone call. Bank-transfer orders are confirmed after we have checked your payment. Send your payment reference on the order page so we can find it quickly.

Why was my order cancelled automatically?

Orders that are not paid or confirmed in time are cancelled so the items can go back to stock. Just place the order again. If you did send a payment, contact us with your order number and proof of payment.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us before it ships. See the returns page.

What is the return and refund policy?

See the returns and refund policy.

How long does delivery take?

See the shipping policy.

Why do some items come in separate parcels?

Each seller ships their own items, so an order with items from several shops can arrive in more than one parcel.

How do I sell on the marketplace?

Create an account and use the "Become a seller" link. Your shop goes live once the store approves it.
